Recorded sound effects and music used by the OpenAL showcase
=============================================================

Converted by make_sfx_assets.py (Python 3 + ffmpeg) to 16-bit PCM WAV at 48 kHz (mono for 3D
sources, stereo for the 2D music), peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, and embedded in
../../sound_assets_sfx.h. Downloaded from freesound.org; every sound is CC0
(public domain, no attribution required), checked on each sound's page.
